Body Recomposition Core Rules

1. Calorie Deficit vs Maintenance

At 2,000 kcal, you are in a slight deficit that gently burns abdominal fat while providing enough energy for lifting heavy.

2. The 150g Protein Anchor

150g (2.0g per kg of bodyweight) guarantees your muscles don't catabolize while walking and training 3 days a week.

3. Waist Circumference > Scale

Scale weight may stay at 75 kg for weeks while your waist shrinks from 34" to 32". That means you gained muscle and lost belly fat!

4. Flexibility Over Perfection

If carbs are 180g one day and 220g another, don't stress. Total calories and 140–160g protein are 90% of your progress.
